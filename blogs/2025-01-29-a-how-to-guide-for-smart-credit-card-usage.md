Credit cards are great financial tools that offer incredible purchasing power and unmatched convenience. With just a simple swipe or tap, we can make purchases online, in-store, or even halfway across the globe. In fact, according to the Reserve Bank of India, there were 97.9 million active credit cards in India as of December 2023.

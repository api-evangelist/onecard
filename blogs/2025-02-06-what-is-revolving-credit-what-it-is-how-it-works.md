Revolving credit allows you to borrow up to a set limit, repay what you’ve used, and then borrow again, all from the same account. Further, the revolving credit is also used when referring to a credit that can easily change depending on the needs of the borrower at a given time. Revolving credit is thus considered to offer convenience and flexibility to consumers.

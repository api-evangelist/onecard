Managing your finances can often feel like a juggling act, but understanding the importance of your credit utilisation ratio can make a world of difference. A low credit utilisation ratio not only boosts your credit score but also opens doors to better financial opportunities. With a low credit utilisation ratio, you can get lower interest rates on loans and credit cards.
